ABOUT THE WEBSITE

Understanding Your Academic Performance

The Nigerian Grading System is a simple and reliable platform designed to help students calculate and understand their academic grades with ease.

The platform provides grading information and calculation support for students in Secondary Schools, Polytechnics and Universities. It helps students determine their grades, grade points, CGPA and corresponding remarks based on their academic performance.

Whether you want to know if your score is an A, B, C, D, E or F, calculate your grade point, or understand your overall CGPA, this website provides a simple way to get the information you need.
